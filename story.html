Our Story

A guitar.
A coffee.
A counter.

A neighborhood café built around two things we care about: a really good cup of coffee, and the music that plays while you drink it. The drinks are named after our favorite tracks. The decor is full of guitars. The regulars know all the words.

Chapter 01

The name
on the door.

"Axe to Grind" is a wink — the axe is the guitar, and the grind is the coffee. We liked that the name worked two ways. Mostly we liked that it sounded like a place with an opinion.

From day one the goal has been simple: make a really good latte, build the food fresh, and put on a soundtrack that makes the room feel like somewhere people actually want to be. Then keep the doors open and see who walks in.

"We didn't want a coffee shop.
We wanted a green room
for the neighborhood."

— The Owners
Chapter 02

The drinks
got names.

Once you've called a mocha latte the "Mocha Choca Latte, Ya Ya," you can't really go back. The Playlist took shape one drink at a time — a Fleetwood Macchiato here, a Cinnamon Girl there, a build-your-own slot we called "Go Your Own Way."

The kitchen kept up — chicken salad two ways, breakfast sandwiches, waffles called "Elvis" and "Still The One," a cupcake that changes every week. Made to order, every time.

What stays the same

Three things,
every shift.

01 — Craft

Made-to-order, every order.

No pre-built sandwiches. No syrup pumped from a box. The slower way, on purpose.

02 — Hospitality

Friendly faces.

The owners are usually somewhere on the floor. They learn your name by the second visit. It's that kind of place.

03 — Music

Always something on.

The playlist is the whole point. Sometimes loud, sometimes background — always picked by someone who cares.

How it goes

A typical day in.

A.M.

Doors open. Espresso machine warms up.

The first regulars walk in. The first waffle hits the iron. The first record drops.

MID

Kitchen's on full pull.

Chicken salad goes out. The cupcake of the week sells through. Somebody asks what's playing.

P.M.

Italian sodas. Cold brew. Iced lattes.

The afternoon shift — laptops out, sodas full, the playlist a notch louder.

CLOSE

The last cup goes out the door.

Wipe the bar down. Start the playlist over. See you tomorrow.

Today

Come stay a while.

The kettle's hot. The cupcakes are out. There's a chair by the window with your name on it.
