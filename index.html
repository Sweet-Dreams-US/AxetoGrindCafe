The Playlist.

Hand-pulled lattes named after the songs that built the café. Mochas, macchiatos, chai, cold brew, italian sodas — nothing pre-mixed.

02

Made-to-order kitchen.

Breakfast sandwiches on croissant or English muffin, two styles of waffle, chicken salad, BLTs — built when you ask.

03

Sweet stuff.

Scones, a rotating cupcake of the week, and a pastry case that looks different every visit. Always something new on the bar.
